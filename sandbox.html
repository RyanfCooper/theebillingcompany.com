Evaluation environment

Keep real client and company data out.

The sandbox is intended for product evaluation with synthetic information. Prospective users should not upload privileged, confidential, personal or production data. Customer-specific integrations, migration and deployment planning begin only after both sides agree that the product is a potential fit.
