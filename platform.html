Intake & matter management

  • Budget proposals
  • Accrual submissions

Legal e-billing

Bring the invoice, approved rates, billing guidelines, budget position, and matter context into one review path.

  • Manual invoice entry
  • Duplicate detection
  • Rate and guideline validation
  • Line-level adjustments
  • Correction and resubmission history

Set expectations before the bill arrives and run a repeatable close process with firms and internal owners.
