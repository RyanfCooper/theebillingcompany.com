Connected capabilities

Follow the work from first request to final payment.

01

Intake & Matter Management

Transform business requests and service-of-process into structured matters, then guide work through ownership, review, and resolution.

  • Configurable intake forms
  • Structured milestones & closure requirements
  • Governed fields & standardized taxonomies
  • Team roles, tasks, deadlines, and documents
  • Pinned records & quick-action menus
  • Granular access controls & immutable audit history
02

Outside Counsel Collaboration

Provide law firms with a dedicated workspace for each client relationship, with access strictly limited to their assigned matters and billing.

  • Firm, office, and timekeeper profiles
  • Secure matter updates & shared work product
  • Office-level permissions & client access grants
  • Searchable rate cards & annual rate reviews
  • Monthly budget & accrual submissions
  • Post-matter performance scorecards
03

Intelligent Legal eBilling

Review invoices against approved rate cards, billing guidelines, and matter budgets. AI flags discrepancies, proposes adjustments, and routes exceptions through configured approval thresholds.

  • LEDES, PDF, and automated intake
  • One-click line-item adjustments & history
  • Automated guideline & block-billing detection
  • Credit memos & shadow invoice handling
  • Multi-tier threshold escalation
  • Saved drafts & guided firm resubmissions
04

Budgets & Spend Management

Set matter spend targets and enforce department, practice-group, and entity caps. Collect outside counsel accruals through a predictable month-end close.

  • Matter, phase, and task budgets
  • Real-time remaining budget tracking
  • Outside counsel budget proposals
  • Alternative Fee Arrangements (AFAs)
  • Spend pacing & burn rate analytics
  • Automated accrual requests & close workflows
05

Purchase Orders & Accounts Payable

Generate purchase orders with automatic PO assignment, monitor committed spend against balance caps, and deliver approved invoice batches cleanly to Accounts Payable.

  • PO requests & internal approval thresholds
  • Real-time invoice-to-PO balance tracking
  • Entity & cost-center billing routing
  • Two-way accounting & ERP sync options
  • Audit-ready Accounts Payable batch exports
  • Automated multi-currency rate conversion
06

Spend Intelligence & Reporting

Build cross-department reports with interactive filters, custom columns, and live charts. Use natural-language prompts to compose spend queries with zero calculation drift.

  • Cross-record custom report builder
  • Role-based workspace & dashboard sharing
  • Automated report scheduling & delivery
  • Interactive visualizations & drill-down charts
  • One-click Excel, CSV, and PDF exports
  • AI-assisted query builder with cited sources
07

Enterprise Administration & Governance

Standardize core legal workflows while configuring custom fields, approval routing, notification rules, and role permissions to fit your organization.

  • Bulk CSV imports with pre-validation previews
  • Dynamic cascading picklists & taxonomy
  • Targeted department broadcasts & alerts
  • Automated data integrity & reconciliation health
  • Custom notification triggers & delivery logs
  • Granular user roles & delegation controls
