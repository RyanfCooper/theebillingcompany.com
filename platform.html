  • In-house legal workspace
  • Outside counsel portal
  • Legal finance and AP controls
  • Governed configuration and document tools

Connected capabilities

Follow the matter all the way through.

01

Intake & matter management

Turn legal requests and service-of-process events into structured matters, then guide the work through ownership, review, and closure.

  • Configurable request forms
  • Governed fields and managed lists
  • Pending phase and required closure details
  • People, tasks, dates, and files
  • Favorites and recently visited records
  • Matter access controls and audit history
02

Outside counsel collaboration

Give firms a dedicated workspace for each client relationship, with controlled access to the matters and billing tasks they handle.

  • Firm, office, and billing identities
  • Searchable timekeeper and rate-review directories
  • Matter updates and shared files
  • Budget and accrual submissions
  • Office-level access and client grants
  • Post-matter firm scorecards
03

Legal e-billing

Review invoices with approved rates, guidelines, budgets, and matter context at hand. Route exceptions through configured approval thresholds.

  • LEDES, PDF, and manual intake
  • Duplicate and guideline validation
  • Line-level adjustments and history
  • Threshold-based escalation
  • Shadow and credit invoice types
  • Saved drafts and guided resubmission
04

Budgets & accruals

Plan matter spend and monitor it within department, practice-group, and entity ceilings. Collect firm estimates through a repeatable close process.

  • Matter and phase budgets
  • Alternative fee arrangements
  • Practice-group and entity rollups
  • Fiscal calendars and burn views
  • Firm budget proposals
  • Accrual requests and close controls
05

Purchase orders & the AP loop

Request a purchase order with a firm, amount, and purpose. Approve it in ELM with an automatic PO number, then track invoice commitments and the remaining balance. Accounting integration is optional.

  • Simple requests and internal approvals
  • Optional accounting decision callbacks
  • Invoice-to-PO selection and balances
  • Auditable AP handoffs and delivery review
  • Office billing identities and payment history
  • Currency rates, feeds, and CSV upload
06

Self-service reporting & assisted analysis

Build a report across related records, choose columns and filters, and inspect the results. A text-to-report assistant can help compose the report; financial calculations remain deterministic.

  • Cross-object report builder
  • Live previews and charts
  • Private, role, and workspace sharing
  • Excel, CSV, and PDF exports
  • Scheduled reports with recipient access checks
  • Modular dashboards and cited review flags
07

Documents & generation

Generate documents from governed Word templates on matter, vendor, and contact records. Connect existing legal document stores during implementation.

  • Merge-tag template library
  • Test merges before publishing templates
  • Word output and PDF conversion
  • Record-linked document generation
  • iManage, NetDocuments, and SharePoint adapters
  • DMS tenant validation during setup
08

Configuration & administration

Keep standard workflows consistent while adapting fields, lists, defaults, and communications to your team.

  • CSV import previews and auditable results
  • Data health and reconciliation checks
  • Managed cascading lists
  • Email rules, delivery history, and recovery
  • Targeted login announcements
  • User profiles, preferences, and delegation
09

Personal internal timesheets

Capture your working week against accessible matters and standard internal activities. An administrator enables timekeeping when your team needs it.

  • Monday-to-Sunday weekly entry
  • Decimal hours or hours and minutes
  • Optional private daily notes
  • Automatic row carryover
  • Saved history and conflict recovery
  • Personal access and a mobile scrolling grid
10

Donna record assistance

Ask Donna to find a matter, firm, or invoice, then open the source record to continue your work. Answers use records you can access and server-calculated financial values.

  • Read-only chat in the legal workspace
  • Record search and explicit selection
  • Links back to source records
  • Current access checks on each lookup
  • Clear handling of missing financial data
  • Voice and action-taking remain in development
