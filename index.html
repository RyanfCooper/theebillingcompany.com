Security that holds up in the database

Access control isn't a checkbox in our UI — it's enforced where the data lives.

Row-level isolation

Tenant, matter, and vendor walls are PostgreSQL row-level security policies. A firm's session physically cannot read another firm's rows — or your internal ones.

Append-only audit

Every material change lands in an append-only audit ledger from the very first migration. Who, what, when — and it can't be quietly rewritten.

Least-privilege by default

The application itself runs on a least-privilege database login. Admin paths, approval gates and role policies are enforced server-side, not styled client-side.

AI where it earns its keep

Ask a question, get a runnable report. Get a briefing on your own matters when you sign in. Draft invoices extracted from PDFs for a human to confirm. AI in this product assists the professional — it never replaces the review, and computed figures stay computed, not generated.

Zero-data-retention model policy, US-hosted inference, and per-tenant provider control.
