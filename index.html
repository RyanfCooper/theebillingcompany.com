Security by architecture

Legal and financial data deserve controls beneath the interface.

Tenant, matter and firm boundaries are enforced in PostgreSQL row-level security. Material changes enter an append-only audit ledger. Vendor identities use a separate realm, and sensitive actions remain server-side and attributable.
